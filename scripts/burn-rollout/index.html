ADX burn rollout

Implements the Snapshot vote "Burn an extra 1.00% daily" and prepares for the 9.4M Binance burn.

What this app does

  1. Deploy a StakingPoolLinearBurner for the legacy ADX-STAKING pool through the ERC-2470 factory. It burns the pool's ADX so each share loses 1% of its Oct 8 value per day (linear, zero after 100 days). Because it burns inside the pool, every way out (migrate, rage-leave, unbond) is hit equally.
  2. Deploy the new SupplyController through the factory. It's today's contract plus three changes: ADX burned to 0x0 is excluded from the supply cap, like the multisig's balance already is (otherwise moving the 9.4M out of the multisig puts supply over the cap); a zero-amount mint returns early; and every mintIncentive() calls the burner, so pools are on schedule right before they read their balance.
  3. Give the multisig level-2 governance on it, as it has on the current one.
  4. Point the ADX token at the new SupplyController.
  5. Raise the legacy pool's daily penalty limit to 20% (its maximum). Harmless until the next step, since the pool has no guardian.
  6. Activate: make the burner the pool's guardian and immediately call burn() for the ~4% catch-up, ideally in the same block through a private RPC.
  7. Keep it on schedule: staking activity calls burn() automatically; an hourly cron (or the button) covers quiet periods.
  8. Burn ADX from the 3-of-6 multisig by transferring it to 0x0: one owner submits, two others confirm. The third confirmation executes it.

Every transaction is dry-run with eth_estimateGas from your account before your wallet asks you to sign. Factory deployments get a gas limit sized from a direct deployment (the factory doesn't revert when it runs out of gas) and are checked afterwards. Progress is read from the chain, so reloading is safe. Steps 3–6 are signed by the governance EOA, steps 1, 2 and 7 by any wallet, and step 8 by the multisig owners. The old migrator stays as it is: migrants still lose its fixed 20%, on top of the pool burn.
